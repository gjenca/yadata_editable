% The abstract

The direct limit construction belongs to basic tools used in Universal algebra.
Moreover, G. Gr\"atzer \cite{gra}, page 161 specified problems for the direct limit operator that have not yet been solved. 
The question of what we can get by a direct limit construction from one algebra is very basic in this context and little explored.

Let $\cal A$ be an algebra. 
We denote by $\underrightarrow{\mathbf L} {\cal A}$ the class of all isomorphic copies of direct limits which can be obtained from $\cal A$ 
and we denote by ${\mathbf R}{\cal A}$ the set of all retracts of ${\cal A}$.
Then ${\mathbf R}{\cal A} \subseteq \underrightarrow{\mathbf L} {\cal A}$. 
In general, an algebra  from $\underrightarrow{\mathbf L} {\cal A}$ need not be a retract of $\cal A$, \cite{h2}. 
 
We  say that $\cal A$  is \emph{with easy direct limits} if every algebra from 
$\underrightarrow{\mathbf L} {\cal A}$ is isomorphic to a retract of ${\cal A}$.
Retracts of an algebra with easy direct limits form a direct limit closed class and it is the next motivation
to deal with  direct limit families in which only one algebra occurs.

The goal of the talk is to give a results overview on algebras with easy direct limits.
General algebras as well as several specific algebraic structures as monounary algebras, vector spaces and groups will be mentioned.
